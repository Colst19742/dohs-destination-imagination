Why Join?

In today's modern age, the world increasingly needs creative, outside-the-box thinkers and problem solvers. The current educational system, however, only rewards those who can regurgitate facts on test. At Del Oro, DI was created to remedy this and help students prove that they can be creative problem-solvers.

Additionally, Destination Imagination is useful for colleges and resumes. It shows that the student has taken on a diverse set of challenges outside of academia or athletics alone. In other words, the student proves themself to be well-rounded.

Finally, Destination Imagination offers scholarships at the state level to seniors who have committed themselves to DI. More information about these can be found on the official Destination Imagination website.
