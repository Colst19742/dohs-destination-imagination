Destination Imagination nurtures creativity and teamwork skills, both of which are essential for later life. According to the global DI non-profit:

We enable this transformation by providing challenges rooted in STEAM subjects that inspire unique solutions through the use of the creative process.

For parents, the club is very similar to Odyssey of the Mind. For students, think of the club as an international STEAM competition, similar to a sport.
