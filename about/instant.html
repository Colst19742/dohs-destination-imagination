Instant Challenges

The Instant Challenge portion of competition day is completely spontaneous. Team members have around 3–4 minutes of preparation before presenting their solution to the appraisers. Instant Challenges could involve anything from

  • Creating a skit with no props,
  • Building a structure out of crafts materials to a certain height,
  • Forming a skit with given props, or
  • Constructing a product with crafts materials and advertising it.

Teams can prepare for their one IC by solving practice ICs appraised by the team manager.

Instant Challenges will be used during the application process into the club; they will be appraised by incumbent club officers.
