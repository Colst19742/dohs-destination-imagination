Central Challenges

Destination Imagination's iconic Central Challenges force students to be creative in many different areas. Each includes

  • Artistic elements,
  • Technologic elements,
  • Theatrical elements,
  • Stroy-based elements, and
  • Team Choice Elements.

The central challenge is worth the bulk of the points at competition day. Thus, teams are able to work on their soution to the central challenge for the entire school-year, from September to March.
